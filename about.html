About MaleStripper.net

An independent directory of male strippers for hire across the US and Canada.

MaleStripper.net lists independent male dancers who book their own shows. Each listing has the dancer's city, a photo, his website and his booking number. You pick the dancer, you call him, you set up the party.

Why Independent

When you hire a male stripper through an agency, you often don't know who shows up. Independent dancers are the person in the photos, answer their own phone and set their own rates. That's who this directory is for.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't take bookings or payments.
  • We don't set prices or schedule shows.
  • We aren't an agency and don't employ any dancer listed.

Before You Book

Rates, deposits, travel fees and show rules are set by each dancer. Confirm the date, address, show length and payment with him directly. All events are for guests 18 and older.

Dancers: Get Listed

Independent male entertainers with a working website and booking number can request a listing on the contact page.
